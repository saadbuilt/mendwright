Harbor authority approves a two-year dredging plan

By Mira Okoro October 7, 2026
Email

The harbor authority voted 5 to 2 on Tuesday to approve a dredging plan that will deepen the main channel by two meters over the next two years.

Supporters say the work is overdue. Larger container ships have been waiting offshore for high tide, and two shipping lines have warned they could move routes to other ports.

What changes for ferry routes

The inner-harbor ferries will keep running during the work, with a temporary landing at the north pier from March.

Opponents raised concerns about the eelgrass beds at the mouth of the bay. The authority said it would fund monitoring for five years.
